How an ADID is put together

Path depth gives the level: one segment after /adid/ is a family, two is a variant, three is a single item. Fibre, origin, care and the other details are not in the address. They live in the passport record that the address opens.

How related products are found

Open any record and it shows its neighbourhood, taken from registry links and shared passport data:

  • Up and down: the family a variant belongs to, a family's variants, and a variant's items.
  • Siblings: the other colours and sizes in the same family.
  • Same lot: everything recorded under one batch number. Visible to the manufacturer and to authorities only.
  • Similar: variants of the same category and colour from other families and manufacturers, matched however the values were spelt in the upload.

Every link follows the same access rules as a direct lookup, so individual items are not listed publicly.

From spreadsheet to printed label

  1. Upload

    The product sheet you already keep, as CSV, Excel or JSON.

  2. Match

    The gateway matches your column names to CDM fields, each with a calibrated confidence.

  3. Check

    You confirm or correct each match. Your choices can be saved as a template revision.

  4. Register

    Each family, variant and serialised item receives its ADID and passport record.

  5. Print

    Put the ADID address in the label's QR code. Later corrections update the record behind it.

The regulation this responds to

Passport data must be transferable "without vendor lock-in".
EU Regulation 2024/1781, Art. 10(1)(d)

ADIF uses open identifiers, an open data model and JSON-LD, not one supplier's product codes.

Passports may be required at model, batch or item level.
EU Regulation 2024/1781, Art. 9(2)(d)

ADID addresses the family, variant and item, and records the batch as passport data.

Textiles, in particular garments, are a priority product group.
EU Regulation 2024/1781, Art. 18(5)(c)

The textile delegated act is not yet adopted, so ADIF does not claim compliance with requirements that do not exist yet.
